Under the Hood: Spatial Computer Vision & Kinematics

From 1963 Ivan Sutherland Sketchpad cathode rays to 2026 sub-15ms WebAssembly neural inference, hand tracking evolved from cumbersome glove sensors to pure passive optics. Discover the end-to-end mathematical pipeline that drives this controller.

Pure Client-Side Wasm
01
Optical Ingestion
Zero-copy canvas blit from WebRTC media stream. Transforms camera frames to mirrored coordinate system and normalizes aspect ratio without GPU-to-CPU stalls.
02
BlazePalm Detector
Single-shot neural detector extracts oriented bounding boxes and key anchors (wrist, MCP knuckles) invariant to 3D rotation, roll, and extreme camera tilt.
03
21 3D Joint Regression
MediaPipe Wasm regresses 21 continuous 3D coordinate landmarks ($X, Y, Z$) in relative palm coordinates with metric depth approximation and occlusion recovery.
04
OneEuro & Kinematics
Adaptive dual-cutoff low-pass filter eliminates high-frequency sensor noise during stationary fixation while eliminating lag during fast directional ballistic swipes.
05
Semantic Intent Engine
Vector trigonometry computes finger curl angles (θ), euclidean pinch distances (Δd), and multi-modal actions dispatched across 2D canvas, Web Audio, and MCP.
Mathematical Invariants & Spatial Formulations Kinematics
Finger Curl Angle Vector Math
cos θ = (u · v) / (||u|| · ||v||)
Computed across MCP → PIP → DIP → Tip vectors. If θ < 85°, joint is classified as extended; if θ > 120°, finger is curled.
Euclidean Pinch Distance
d(Thumb, Index) = √((x₄ - x₈)² + (y₄ - y₈)² + (z₄ - z₈)²)
Normalized by palm span ||p_wrist - p_middle_mcp|| to guarantee distance scale-invariance regardless of user distance from camera.
Adaptive OneEuro Filter
f_c = f_{c,min} + β · |dx/dt|
Dynamically modulates low-pass cutoff frequency based on instantaneous landmark speed. Eliminates stationary hand tremors while preventing swipe lag.
Evolution of Spatial Hand Tracking (1963 – 2026) 63-Year Journey
1963 — Ivan Sutherland's Sketchpad MIT Lincoln Lab
The birth of direct human-computer graphical interaction. Used a physical light gun on a CRT monitor, laying the foundation for spatial coordinate input and computer graphics primitives.
1981 — Lucas-Kanade Optical Flow Differential Tracking
Bruce Lucas and Takeo Kanade introduced differential image velocity estimation. Assumed spatial constancy to track visual motion vectors across video frames without markers.
2001 — Viola-Jones Real-Time Cascades Integral Images
Paul Viola and Michael Jones demonstrated 15 FPS real-time facial feature detection on commodity Pentium III CPUs using AdaBoost feature selection and integral image computation.
2010 — Microsoft Kinect & Structured Light Infrared Depth
PrimeSense IR speckle projection combined with Jamie Shotton's randomized decision forests enabled markerless whole-body skeletal tracking in consumer living rooms.
2020 — Google MediaPipe Hands & BlazePalm On-Device ML
Fan Zhang, Valentin Bazarevsky et al. published the BlazePalm + 2.5D landmark regression pipeline. Real-time 21-joint articulated hand pose detection running entirely client-side on mobile GPUs and WebAssembly.
2024–2026 — Zero-Install In-Browser Spatial Kinematics & MCP Current Era
Full WebAssembly SIMD execution with zero-latency browser rendering, Solfeggio Web Audio synthesis, interactive shader filter cutouts, dual-hand spatial manipulation, and bi-directional Model Context Protocol (MCP) agent interoperability.
